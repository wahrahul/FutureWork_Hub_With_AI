# FutureWork Hub with AI — 3-minute demo video

## Recording setup

- Run `npm start` and open `http://localhost:4200`.
- Use a clean browser window at 1440×900 or similar.
- For the AI moments, configure Gemini from the sparkle icon, or use the local fallback behavior.
- Use sample content only; do not enter confidential work data.

## Timeline and narration

### 0:00–0:20 — Opening: one calm workspace

**On screen:** Start on the Dashboard. Slowly move across the queue, mood score, focus timer, meeting notes, and Knowledge Hub cards.

**Narration:**

> Meet FutureWork Hub with AI: a private productivity workspace designed around human pace. It brings tasks, mood check-ins, meeting notes, wellbeing, and saved knowledge into one quiet, focused view.

### 0:20–0:50 — Personal check-in

**On screen:** Open **Mood**. Select **Stressed** or **Curious**, then enter: “I have several competing deadlines and need help deciding what to do first.” Select **Reflect with AI**.

**Narration:**

> I’ll start with a quick check-in. The user stays in control: they can select a mood or describe what is taking up space. The app turns that reflection into a lightweight personal signal—not a diagnosis or a performance score—and keeps manual check-ins in charge.

### 0:50–1:20 — Turn the day into a clear next step

**On screen:** Open **Tasks**. Add two or three tasks, for example: “Review launch feedback,” “Prepare stakeholder update,” and “Book customer follow-up.” Assign projects. Click **Prioritize with AI**.

**Narration:**

> Next, I capture the work in front of me. Tasks are grouped by project, easy to edit, and easy to complete. With Gemini connected, prioritization uses the task context and current check-in to suggest a manageable order. Without Gemini, the local fallback keeps the core workflow available offline.

### 1:20–1:55 — Make meetings useful after they end

**On screen:** Open **Meeting notes**. Paste a short sample:

> The team agreed to move the pilot to Thursday. Maya will confirm the customer list. Arun will update the dashboard before the next review. The open question is whether support needs a new runbook.

Click **Summarize & save**, then show the saved note and next steps.

**Narration:**

> After a meeting, I paste rough notes instead of rewriting them. FutureWork Hub turns them into a concise record with decisions and action items, then saves the result in the workspace so the thread is easy to pick up later.

### 1:55–2:25 — Protect attention

**On screen:** Open **Wellbeing**. Start the 5-minute reset, then show the focus timer and the context-sensitive wellbeing message. Optionally pause the timer.

**Narration:**

> Productivity here includes recovery. The wellbeing space offers a short reset or a longer focus session, with gentle guidance based on the user’s check-in. The goal is not to optimize every minute—it is to make the next useful step easier to take.

### 2:25–2:50 — Find what was saved

**On screen:** Open **Knowledge hub** and search for “pilot” or “dashboard”. Show the matching task and meeting result.

**Narration:**

> When I need to reconnect with past work, the Knowledge Hub searches saved tasks and meeting notes together. Results stay local by default, and Gemini-powered semantic search is available when explicitly configured.

### 2:50–3:00 — Privacy close

**On screen:** Return to Dashboard. Briefly show the privacy note and the Gemini settings control.

**Narration:**

> FutureWork Hub keeps work on this device by default. Camera, typing rhythm, voice cues, and Gemini are opt-in, and the app makes those boundaries visible. It is a productivity tool built to help people work with more clarity—and a little more room to breathe.
